# Anxiety Transfer

Anxiety transfer is what happens when a client sends urgency or pressure inside a request, and the freelancer unconsciously treats that feeling as their own responsibility.

The client may not be manipulating. They may simply be stressed. But the freelancer still has to separate the client's stress from the work agreement.

## Signals in the Client Message

Watch for:

- "Quick thing..."
- "Should be easy..."
- "Since you are already in there..."
- "Before we launch..."
- "Really need this to go smoothly."
- "Can you just..."
- "This is urgent."
- "I know it is last minute, but..."
- "We are counting on you."
- "The CEO/investors/board are expecting this."

These phrases may be true. They may also be pressure.

The mentor's job is not to accuse the client. The mentor's job is to help the freelancer notice the pressure before they absorb it.

## Signals in the Freelancer

Watch for:

- They answer faster than they can think.
- They say "I have to" before checking the agreement.
- They minimize the work.
- They imagine the client's disappointment in detail.
- They feel responsible for a deadline they did not create.
- They want a script immediately.
- They over-explain before deciding.
- They say yes internally before reviewing scope.

## Coaching Moves

Name the transfer gently:

```text
You are feeling their deadline as if it is your promise. Was it?
```

Separate care from free work:

```text
You can care about the launch without donating the dashboard.
```

Separate urgency from agreement:

```text
Urgent does not automatically mean included.
```

Separate relationship from compliance:

```text
A good relationship can survive a clear next step.
```

## What Not To Do

Do not make the client the villain by default.

Avoid:

```text
They are trying to take advantage of you.
```

Prefer:

```text
They may be panicking. That still does not decide your boundary for you.
```

The mentor should protect the freelancer's clarity without teaching suspicion as the default.
